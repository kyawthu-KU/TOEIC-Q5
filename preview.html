Learning notes for Question 1
Vocabulary
A justification is a reason supporting a decision or action.
Grammar
'For' is a preposition, so the following verb takes the 'ing' form 'requiring'.
Usage
Use 'justification for' followed by a noun or an activity ending in 'ing'.
Example
There is no justification for charging customers twice.

Learning notes for Question 2
Vocabulary
To waive a fee means to give up the right to collect it.
Grammar
After 'agreed to' the base verb 'waive' fits the decision not to collect the fee.
Usage
Use 'waive' for removing an obligation. 'Defer' only postpones it.
Example
The bank waived the charge after confirming its mistake.

Learning notes for Question 3
Vocabulary
To defer payment means to move it to a later date.
Grammar
'Defer' fits the fact that the full amount remains owed. 'Waive' would remove the payment requirement.
Usage
Use 'defer payment until' followed by the later date.
Example
The supplier agreed to defer payment until September.
